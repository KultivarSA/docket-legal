Docket Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Docket is an offline-first quoting and invoicing app for South African artisans, published by Kultivar SA Pty Ltd (CIPC registration 2026/429365/07), a South African company (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your business data lives on your phone, not on our servers.

What Docket stores, and where

Everything you create in Docket — quotes, invoices, clients, catalogue items, expenses, receipts, vehicle logbook trips, job plans, photos and your business profile (including banking details you choose to show on invoices) — is stored in a local database on your device. We do not run a server, we have no account system, and we cannot see, access or recover your data.

Your phone’s own backup. If Google backup is switched on in your phone’s settings, Android also keeps a copy of your Docket records, your logo and letterhead images, and the app’s settings in your own Google account. That is what brings them back if you reinstall Docket or set up a new phone. The copy is made and kept by Google, under your Google account and Google’s terms; we cannot see or access it. On Android 9 and newer, with a screen lock set, it is encrypted with your screen lock. Receipt, odometer and vehicle photos are not included. You can switch this backup off, or delete it, in your phone’s backup settings.

When you move to a new phone using Android’s own transfer (by cable or Wi-Fi), Docket’s data comes across too, photos included.

Backup & restore in Docket’s Settings exports everything, receipt photos included, to one file that you store wherever you choose (email, cloud drive, etc.). Whatever service you send it to is governed by that service’s own privacy policy.

What leaves your device

  • Crash reports. If the app crashes or hits an internal error, a diagnostic report is sent to Sentry (sentry.io), our crash-reporting provider. It contains technical information such as the device model, OS version, app version and a stack trace of the error. It is not intended to contain your business data. We use these reports solely to find and fix bugs.
  • Text recognition model. Receipt and odometer scanning use Google ML Kit. Recognition runs on your device — your photos are never uploaded. On first installation Google Play services downloads the recognition model to your phone.
  • Purchases (Docket Pro). Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Our billing provider, RevenueCat, receives purchase tokens and an anonymous app-generated identifier to validate your subscription. We never see your card details.
  • Where you found Docket. The first time Docket runs, it asks Google Play how it was installed. If you installed it from one of our links or QR codes (on a flyer at a supplier’s counter, for example), the link’s source, medium and campaign tags are sent to RevenueCat with the same anonymous identifier, so we can tell which of our promotions work. They contain no personal information, and nothing is sent if you found Docket some other way.
  • Google Play. Docket asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, and now and then asks it to show its own review card. Both go through the Play Store app on your phone, under Google’s privacy policy. We receive nothing from either, apart from any review you choose to post.
  • Things you choose to send. Sharing a PDF on WhatsApp, exporting a backup, sending an accountant pack — these only happen when you tap the button, and the content goes to the app you pick.

Apart from the above, nothing leaves your phone: Docket has no analytics or advertising tools, shows no ads, and we do not sell or share any personal information.

Permissions Docket asks for

  • Camera / photos — photographing receipts and odometers, and choosing a business logo or vehicle photo. Images stay on your device, apart from your logo and letterhead in your phone’s own backup, described above.
  • Notifications — local reminders for invoice due dates, quote expiry and SARS deadlines. These are scheduled on your phone, not from a server.

Your data, your control

Deleting the app deletes all Docket data on the device, except backup files you exported yourself. If Google backup is on, the copy in your Google account stays there, under Google’s control, until you delete it in your phone or Google account settings, or Google removes it. There is nothing for us to delete on any server. If you sell or replace your phone, use Backup & restore in Settings, or Android’s own transfer, to move your data.

Children

Docket is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially we will update it here and note it in the app’s release notes. Questions or requests: docket@kultivar.io.
